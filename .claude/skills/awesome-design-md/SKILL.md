# Awesome DESIGN.md (VoltAgent)

`design-md/<brand>/` holds a DESIGN.md per site: colour tokens, typography, spacing, components and tone.
Use them as references, not templates: read two or three that suit the subject, take the principles
(scale, rhythm, restraint, colour proportions) and apply them in the project's own brand.
Source: https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md (see LICENSE).
