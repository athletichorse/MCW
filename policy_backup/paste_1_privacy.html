MIM Creative Wear ("we", "us") runs mimcreativewear.com. This policy explains what personal information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us when you order or contact us: name, email, phone number, delivery and billing address, and order details.
  • Payment details are handled by our payment providers through Shopify's secure checkout. We do not store your full card number.
  • Information collected automatically when you browse: device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed, and cookies.

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, including exchanges and refunds
  • To answer your questions
  • To send marketing emails if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand how our website is used, improve it, and measure and show our ads, using tools such as Google Analytics and Google Ads
  • To prevent fraud and meet legal obligations

Sharing

We share information only with the services that help us run the store: Shopify (our store platform), payment providers, delivery companies, and analytics and advertising partners such as Google. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart and currency, remember your preferences, and measure site and ad performance. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work without them.

Keeping your information

We keep order information for as long as needed for our records, accounting and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by emailing mim@mimcreativewear.com.
