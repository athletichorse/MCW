Exchanges

If you are not happy with your piece, you can exchange it within 2 days of receiving your order.

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and unused, with all tags and packaging in place.
  • To arrange an exchange, email mim@mimcreativewear.com or call customer service on +201102220008 with your order number.
  • You pay the delivery costs for an exchange.
  • Requests made after 2 days may be declined. If we accept a late exchange, an extra fee of EGP 50 is added to the delivery charge.

Faulty, damaged or wrong items

Every piece is quality checked before it ships. If you receive a piece with a manufacturing fault, damage, or the wrong item or size, tell us within 2 days of delivery at mim@mimcreativewear.com. We will replace it if it is in stock, or refund the price of the item once we receive it back.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued after we receive and inspect the item, to your original payment method. For cash on delivery orders we will agree the refund method with you.

Not covered

  • Items that have been worn, washed or altered
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Small variations in print, colour or size: every piece is printed by hand, so no two are exactly alike
