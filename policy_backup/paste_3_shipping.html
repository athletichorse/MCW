Shipping

We ship across Egypt and internationally. Every order is confirmed by email with its order number. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next working day.

Shipping in Egypt (EGP)

  • Cairo and Giza: Standard (2 to 5 working days) 85, free on orders over 2,999. Express (1 to 2 working days) 120.
  • Rest of Egypt (Alexandria, Delta, Canal cities, Upper Egypt, Red Sea, Sinai and Matrouh): Standard (2 to 5 working days) 85. Express (1 to 2 working days) 120.

Shipping outside Egypt (EGP)

  • Orders up to 4,000: 1,200
  • Orders over 4,000: 3,200

International delivery times depend on the destination and are confirmed by email after you order. Any customs duties or import taxes are paid by the customer.

The exact shipping cost is always shown at checkout before you pay.

Payment

Pay by card online, or cash on delivery within Egypt.
