Reading: shop homepage for a Cairo label of hand-printed women's wear; one job: get the visitor into the pieces
(shop new in / shop by print). Stack: Shopify Dawn-based theme, Liquid sections, plain CSS/JS. Brand fixed:

gardens, among Fustat pots), product photos for every piece.

## Inventory (only MIM has these)
1. Name: "mim" = the Arabic letter م; logo is a feather and the handwritten word.
2. Real leaves laid on cloth — eco print and botanical print use leaves from Cairo gardens.
3. Cyanotype: the Cairo sun prints the cloth Prussian blue; exposure is minutes, set by the day's light.
4. Carved wooden blocks for block print; four techniques + hand-painted.
5. Studio/office in Fustat (Old Cairo, the potters' quarter, opposite Mamlouk); second shop at CSA Maadi.
6. The photographs: the founder modelling among terracotta pots and garden leaves — the same leaves that print.
7. Every piece one of a kind (print lands differently each time); sizes XS–3XL and relaxed 1–3.
8. Founder May: tour guide turned maker — Saqqara, papyrus, tree-of-life motifs in the prints.
9. Cash on delivery in Egypt, free delivery over 2,500 EGP in Cairo & Giza, ships worldwide.
10. Weather-aware shelf already exists (Open-Meteo): the day's temperature decides what to wear.
